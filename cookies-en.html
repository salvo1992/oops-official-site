Cookies and local storage

Updated 30 October 2026

Important: data stored by the Android app on a device is not automatically a browser cookie used by this website.

1. This website

The static pages included in this release do not load analytics scripts, ad pixels or proprietary tracking cookies. The website host may process technical request logs for delivery and security.

2. Local app storage

The app may save progress and preferences on your device. The provided technical texts mention oops.save.v1 and oops.session.v1; verify their actual use in the released app source code.

3. Advertising SDKs

If enabled, Google AdMob may process device identifiers and technical information under applicable consent and privacy rules.

4. Controls

Manage app preferences in Settings, device data through Android controls, and account-related data using the account deletion page.
