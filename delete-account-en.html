Delete your OOPS! account

Updated 30 October 2026

You can request deletion of your OOPS! account and associated personal data.

1. In the app

If the installed version includes Account → Delete account, follow the on-screen steps. The actual implementation must be verified in the released build.

2. By email

Contact thevikingoftheweb@gmail.com using the email linked to your account when possible. Subject: OOPS account deletion.

We may ask for proportionate identity verification. Never send passwords.

Prepare email

3. Data deleted

Following a verified request, we will delete account data and associated information no longer necessary, such as profile, synced progress and related scores, subject to actual technical implementation.

4. Possible retention

Purchase or legal records may need to be retained as required by law. Google processes certain information independently under its own notices.

5. Timing

Requests are handled within the time limits required by data protection law, generally one month unless a lawful extension applies. See the privacy notice.
